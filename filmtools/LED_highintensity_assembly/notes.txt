this is an LED assembly for motion picture projection

i am using a high intensity led with a 22amp constant current buck converter, and instead of using
the current control settings on the buck I am replacing the PWM signal from the onboard microcontroller
with that of a digital multiblade shutter emulation. 

this driver has a very nice slew characteristic because of some of the filters that get the PWM signal ready to be listened to by
the buck driver chip. 

if you are not interested in driving an led with this sort of stroboscopic action you may use the driver out of the box using the 
external controller to dim the bulb (you may also talk to it VIA serial if you prefer to forgo the LCD readout.) you will end up with 
an image with a higher intensity and throw than a traditional 1200w projector bulb.
